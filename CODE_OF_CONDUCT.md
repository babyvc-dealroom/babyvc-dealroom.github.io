# Code of Conduct

Baby VC Deal Room is a learning project. People join it to learn, often for the first time, about a field where information asymmetry is the norm. We want this community to be the opposite: open, patient and generous with knowledge.

## Our standards

We adopt the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as our code of conduct. In short:

- Be respectful and welcoming, whatever someone's background or level of experience.
- Explain, do not show off: a beginner's question is a chance to improve the docs.
- Give and accept constructive feedback; focus on what is best for learners.
- No harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes, or publishing others' private information.

In games run with this software, the same spirit applies: negotiate hard, but bluff only on intentions, never on facts, and never pressure players outside the game.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers at **[conduct contact to be set by the maintainers]**. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions and temporarily or permanently ban contributors who break this code, following the Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines.
